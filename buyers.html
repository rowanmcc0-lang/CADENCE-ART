Drop I — Land & Labor

The people
behind the work.

Three makers from Vermont. This is who made what you're about to see.

1

Artist Name

Photography · Northeast Kingdom

Working from a small farm in Orleans County. Their photographs are made on large format film — slow, deliberate, tied to one landscape returning across seasons.

2

Artist Name

Printmaking · Burlington

Their prints begin as field drawings made while walking. By the time the work reaches the press it's abstracted, but the landscape stays in it — in the line weight, in what gets left out.

3

Artist Name

Ceramics · Montpelier

Objects made for use — bowls, cups, vessels. The clay comes from Vermont when she can get it. The forms come from thinking about what it means to make something functional that still holds a presence.
