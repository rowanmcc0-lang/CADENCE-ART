Questions

A few things
people ask.

What if nothing sells?

You owe nothing. No fees, no listing charges. We only make money when you do.

Do I give up my work upfront?

No. Your work stays with you until a piece sells. Unsold work never leaves your hands.

How does pricing work?

You tell us what you want to make from each piece. We set the retail price from there. We might share what similar work has sold for to help you think it through, but the number starts with you.

I've never sold before. Can I apply?

Yes. Career stage doesn't factor into selection. The work and your Vermont connection do.

How often are drops?

Two to three per year, seasonally. Drop II is this summer.
